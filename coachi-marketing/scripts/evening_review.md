# Evening Review Script

## Objective
Review what was published, what was learned, and what should be repeated or dropped tomorrow.

## Review Questions
1. Which message angle drove the most interest?
2. Which hook best expressed Coachi's "coach that's actually there" position?
3. Did today's content create clear reasons to visit `Coachi.no`?
4. Which ideas felt generic or too close to competitor language?

## Required Output
- 1 short daily summary
- 1 pattern to repeat
- 1 pattern to stop
- 1 experiment to queue for tomorrow

## Output Location
- Save the review under `outputs/daily/`
- Move reusable learnings into `outputs/weekly/` when they repeat
