Everyday Runner Lab Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Everyday Runner Lab is a web app for runners and fitness creators who sign in with TikTok, create or preview original running education content, choose post settings, and publish creator-approved content through the TikTok Content Posting API.

Information We Process

When a creator signs in to Everyday Runner Lab and connects a TikTok account, TikTok may provide basic account information needed to identify the connected account and publish approved content. This can include account identity, uploaded media, post settings selected by the creator, and publishing status.

How We Use Information

Everyday Runner Lab uses TikTok information only to connect the creator's account, show the correct posting account, upload original media after consent, publish posts, and manage publishing status.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed by TikTok and the publishing workflow providers needed to operate the creator workspace.

Data Retention

We keep only the information needed to operate the publishing workflow and remove it when it is no longer required or when the connected account is disconnected.

Contact

For Everyday Runner Lab privacy questions, contact the account owner through the active Everyday Runner Lab social profile.
