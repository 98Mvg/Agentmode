# Veo Watch Confusion Video Pack — 2026-04-13

## Objective
Create one growth-first, non-branded runner-pain short for TikTok and Instagram using Veo 3.1 as the source-footage engine and the existing shared Coachi render pipeline for both platforms.

## Why This Angle
- it starts with a real runner pain, not product explanation
- it creates comments through contradiction
- it works without brand context
- it can grow followers before introducing Coachi later

## Core Hook
Your easy run feels easy. Your watch says you are doing it wrong.

## Variant Intent
- `A` -> strongest recognition
- `B` -> strongest interruption
- `C` -> strongest confession/comment bait

## Posting Order
1. post TikTok first
2. compare early retention/comments across the three hooks
3. post the strongest hook to Instagram Reels
4. use Stories after the Reel:
   - poll: `pace` / `heart rate`
   - question box: `What number on your watch messes with your head?`

## Success Criteria
- hook reads in the first `2s`
- viewers recognize themselves in the contradiction
- comments reveal confusion, disagreement, or confession
- saves and shares come from the pain feeling personal
- profile curiosity rises without any in-video brand mention
