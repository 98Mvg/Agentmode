## 3 Key Learnings
1. The strongest repeated message is still the simplest one: Coachi wins when it is framed as "a coach in your ear during the run," not as a bundle of AI features. The X thread, TikTok script, and Instagram post all get clearer the moment they lead with no-screen, in-the-moment coaching.
2. The launch drafts prove there are really two different traffic messages: consumer pain relief and founder/tech credibility. The LinkedIn founder story works for trust, but the traffic-driving posts are stronger when they stay on runner uncertainty, not on the build story.
3. The current launch content sometimes stacks too many promises in one post: Apple Watch sync, voice coaching, post-run conversation, AI, free, no account. That makes the message broader, but less clickable. The best traffic message is one pain, one promise, one CTA.

## 2 Things To Double Down On
1. During-the-run relief messaging: "You do not need more data. You need someone to tell you whether to slow down or hold pace right now."
2. Apple Watch translation: keep selling interpretation, not telemetry. "Your watch knows your pulse. Coachi tells you what to do with it."

## 2 Things To Stop Doing
1. Stop leading consumer posts with a feature list. Save secondary features like post-workout conversation and language support for follow-up posts or comment threads.
2. Stop relying on generic launch phrases like "AI running coach" or "free on the App Store" as the main hook. They are fine as closing CTA lines, but too weak as the reason to click.

## 1 Theme For Tomorrow's Posts
**The moment you do not know whether to slow down or push.**

Angle examples:
- "Halfway through a run, most people guess. Coachi tells you."
- "The best reason to use Coachi is not after the run. It is the moment you almost get the pace wrong."

## 1 Refined Positioning Angle
**Coachi is the running coach that is there during the run.**

This is sharper than "AI running coach" and more traffic-friendly than "real-time voice coaching" on its own. It translates the product into a felt outcome: less guessing, more confidence, more runs completed correctly.
