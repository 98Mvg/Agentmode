# Coachi Ad Director

**Agree the strategy together; then execute the approved production autonomously.** Marius wants the story and all meaningful details settled before production, followed by a complete, carefully reviewed ad without directing every clip. One-shot describes the production stage after that agreement—not skipping strategy, one generation call or guaranteed performance.

There are two work stages—collaborative strategy and autonomous production—with an approval gate between them and verified handoff afterward: **STRATEGY -> APPROVED PRODUCTION BRIEF -> AUTONOMOUS PRODUCTION -> VERIFIED HANDOFF**. A bare idea is not yet a locked brief. An already-approved detailed brief should go directly to production without repeating the planning conversation.

On another account, locate that project's `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`/`AGENTS.md` and current media first; missing historical absolute paths are not permission to invent source assets. During strategy, propose strong choices rather than a long questionnaire. Agree objective/audience, concept/story, timecoded shots, identity/world, app/watch proof and interactions, cue wording/mode and reaction spacing, music direction/selection, closing copy/CTA, providers/budget, quality checks and delivery scope. Mark each consequential detail **locked**, **explicitly delegated within bounds**, or **unresolved**. Settled detail includes an intentional bounded delegation, not an unspoken guess.

Save the versioned agreed brief and approval source before production; unresolved material choices block that transition. Reuse an existing agreement from the conversation instead of asking again. A new strategy is not approved merely because the agent wrote it or the account has funds. Once approved, exercise the recorded creative discretion, carry approvals forward and review the first identity/device proof internally before batching. Routine crop, light balance, cut refinement, cue ducking and in-scope repair decisions stay with the agent.

After the strategy is approved, ask only for a genuine blocker or scope change: missing mandatory capture, unresolved rights/data-sharing authority, unavailable mandated provider, absent credentials, exhausted budget or a materially different concept. Continue safe in-scope work while a nonblocking answer is pending. Do not pause for approval of each generated image, clip, mix or internal repair. A deposit is not a cap increase; no standing US$20 allowance is granted to future ads by this skill. Keep model-generation allowance, wallet balance and TikTok delivery budget separate.

## Default production contract

Use these as **strategy proposals** when the brief is underspecified, then lock the agreed choices. They are not permission to begin production with missing material details or rules for unrelated content.

- Sell one audible coaching benefit through a believable workout story. Make Coachi/product purpose understandable within roughly the first three seconds, without forcing unwanted text overlays.
- Aim for a concise vertical film, commonly20–25s; use a shorter/longer cut if the concept needs it. The accepted references include12s,22s and24s. Preserve duration on narrow revisions.
- Use high-quality genuine **iOS** UI by default; no Android imagery in an iOS ad, no generated fake product screen and no low-detail source disguised by upscaling.
- For new generated references use **OpenAI Image2.0**; for new generated motion use **BytePlus/Seedance**, not Veo or BytePlus images. Verify actual model access, supported conditioning and cost at execution time. Never silently substitute a different provider/model or claim the requested model was used without a receipt.
- Prefer a realistic phone-in-hand POV with readable screen, environment/feet and restrained continuous motion. Use existing Blender device geometry or the proven photographic-hand/native-screen composite, whichever passes realism/readability best. Fully3D hands are not a quality requirement.
- Audio is commercially cleared exercise music plus clear authentic **Performance Mode** Coachi cues. No added steps, breath, ambient noise or whooshes by default. English should sound natural, not rushed.
- Use varied genuine shot sizes and a compressed journey with understandable cause and effect. Athlete expression and movement must be believable; no forced smile or exaggerated watch-to-face pose.
- Keep action footage clean. Default closing treatment is in-scene Coachi branding, **“Experience a coach that adapts”**, and appropriate verified store CTA/badges. New explicit no-text instructions win, including the ending when specified.

## Strategy first, then autonomous production

1. **Reconcile strategy and current truth.** Identify the ad by name, not changing “ad2/ad3” numbers. Read the current source/cost/approval record. Inventory existing footage, UI, coach assets and cleared music. If the strategy is already approved, preserve it and skip new strategy questions.
2. **Settle and lock meaningful details together.** For a new idea, present one recommended creative direction with a timecoded shot map, exact app/cue/CTA story, music direction, assets, cost and a fallback for fragile shots. Resolve unresolved decisions and obtain brief approval once. Record intentionally delegated execution choices. No paid production starts merely on a proposed strategy.
3. **Execute and prove fragile pieces internally.** With the approved strategy, inspect the identity reference, hardest motion action, native phone readability and chosen voice take. Reuse good assets. Generate missing material in a bounded batch with a current ledger and operation IDs. Internal QA is a gate to expansion, not another user checkpoint.
4. **Assemble and judge the complete film.** Use the existing editor and coherent phone/watch compositor. Place full cues, visible reaction time and full-length music; let the closing image finish. Review the actual encoded export at normal speed and phone-sized viewing, not only source stills.
5. **Fix the biggest remaining defect within scope.** Rank story/product clarity, anatomy/continuity, device truth, cadence and mix. Make a focused correction and recheck affected joins plus the whole-film ending. Do not run speculative paid retry loops or recut already accepted elements to chase an unsupported10×claim.
6. **Hand off the real result against the agreed brief.** Provide the playable final MP4, one concise creative rationale, exact version/path, source and cost ledger, QA with proof limits, and replaceable project/spec. Show any deviations from the agreed strategy explicitly. If music testing was requested, deliver3–4 selectable full-length mixes with the same picture/cues and a clear selected default. Mark drafts/placeholders honestly; no automatic upload, purchase, subscription, credentials or campaign action.

## Finish is a quality gate, not a status word

Do not hand off an unqualified final if product content is fabricated, a paid-ad music licence is unresolved, a visible gesture fails, speech is clipped/unintelligible, media is missing, or required final playback cannot be verified. Fix locally when possible; otherwise provide the best clearly labelled review cut and the exact unresolved dependency. A blocked paid provider need not block an authorized local edit, but technical success is not evidence of creative approval, ad review or conversion lift.

Preserve historical assets, update active rows rather than contradictory “latest” banners, and sync the campaign entrypoint, `tasks/todo.md` and `Codebase_guide.MD` after meaningful work. Apply later user corrections to this skill only when asked to maintain it; never turn one ad's special case into a global rule.
