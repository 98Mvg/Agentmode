# Lessons

- 2026-09-08: Ad one-shot autonomy begins after a collaboratively agreed strategy with meaningful story, shot, product, voice, music, ending and budget details locked or explicitly delegated. Do not interpret one-shot as silently inventing an unapproved campaign strategy. Once approved, perform routine production and refinement without clip-by-clip approval; ask only for a real blocker, material deviation or expanded authority.
