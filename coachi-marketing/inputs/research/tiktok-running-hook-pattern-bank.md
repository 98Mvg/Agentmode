## Observed High-Fit Formats

### Beginner Advice List
Observed pattern:
- numbered beginner advice
- "wish I knew" framing
- first-person lessons from experience

Coachi-safe hook templates:
- `5 things I wish I knew`
- `6 things beginner runners miss`
- `My top 5 running tips`
- `5 mistakes new runners make`

Useful for:
- beginner uncertainty
- easy-run pace drift
- zone 2 confusion
