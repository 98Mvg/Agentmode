# Slideshow Creative Corrections

## 2026-05-05 - Simpler Runner Language

Correction:
- `One small surge` is too complicated for beginner-facing TikTok slideshow copy.

Rule:
- Prefer plain runner wording over training jargon.
- Say `you speed up once`, `then again`, or `you go too fast` instead of `surge` when the audience is beginner/intermediate runners.
- Keep slide text instantly understandable without needing training vocabulary.

## 2026-05-04 - Hook Face Direction

Correction:
- The `2026-04-26-watch-stole-the-run-8-slide` hook face/style has stronger viral potential than the cleaner static Coachi avatar look.

Rule:
- Use the `watch-stole-the-run` hook image as the primary slide-1 appearance reference.
- Do not use the clean park-portrait avatar as the default identity reference.
- Keep the strong parts: fitted dark kit, visible sweat, sharper face detail, cinematic warm contrast, serious human expression, and tight social hook framing.
- Do not copy the weak parts by default: mixed lake/mountain world, hands-on-hips pose, visible watch, or watch-checking.
- Watches are allowed only when the hook is explicitly about watch anxiety, and even then only as a small unbranded detail with no readable UI.

## 2026-05-15 - Approved Avatar Worlds

Correction:
- The slideshow system should not invent `city park`, `neighborhood road`, `track`, `treadmill`, or generic street worlds.

Rule:
- Approved slideshow worlds are only `forest`, `mountain`, and `lake`.
- Slide 1 may use the `watch-stole-the-run` runner as appearance reference, but its background must regenerate into one of those three worlds.
- Slides 2-6 must stay in the same approved world as slide 1.
- `lake` is still valid when mountains or hills are visible in the background, as long as the lake/water path remains the primary world.
- World-to-library mapping: `forest` -> `nature_context`, `mountain` -> `hills_effort`, `lake` -> `lake_calm`.
