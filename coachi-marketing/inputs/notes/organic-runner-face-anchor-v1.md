# Organic Runner Face Anchor V1

Use this as the default organic runner identity for Coachi TikTok and Instagram videos.

## Reference Assets
- Full screenshot reference: `content/ads/reference/organic-runner-face-v1-full.png`
- Cropped face reference: `content/ads/reference/organic-runner-face-v1-crop.png`
- Current clean synthetic face reference: `content/ads/reference/organic-runner-face-v2-reference.png`

## Locked Identity
- male runner
- age `25-35`
- lean endurance-athlete build
- tan complexion
- short dark slightly textured hair
- serious, calm, focused expression
- premium but believable everyday runner look

## New Default
The runner face can be visible. The priority is that the face stays consistent across videos.

Use `content/ads/reference/organic-runner-face-v2-reference.png` as the primary `reference_image_assets` entry in new Veo specs whenever the runner face is visible.

For TikTok/Instagram slideshow hook images, the cleaner face reference should not make the output too static or polished. Use `content/slideshows/2026-04-26-watch-stole-the-run-8-slide/slides/source/01-hook.png` as the preferred style reference for the stronger viral face direction: fitted black kit, visible sweat, sharper cheek/brow detail, cinematic warm contrast, and a serious human expression.

Borrow the face/style energy only. Do not copy its mixed lake/mountain world, hands-on-hips pose, or visible watch unless the concept explicitly needs watch anxiety.

## Motion Rules
- one continuous running clip
- natural constant stride
- no doubled step
- no stutter step
- no walking transition
- no unnatural turn
- no fake cold breath or steam
- no heavy background blur
- no watch-checking unless the concept specifically needs it

## Variation Rules
Keep the face, hair, complexion, and body type stable.

Vary these every video:
- workout phase
- camera angle
- route/background
- clothes
- headwear
- eyewear
- shorts color
- weather
- lighting

## Workout Phase Rotation
Rotate the workout moment so every visual does not look like the same scene.

Allowed workout phases:
- pre-workout: preparing before the session, calm anticipation, light warmup, route visible
- during-workout: steady controlled running effort, natural stride, consistent cadence
- post-workout: cooldown or just-finished moment, sweaty, satisfied, grounded

Phase constraints:
- keep it realistic and non-posed
- no fake celebration
- no watch-checking unless the concept explicitly needs it
- no stutter step or unnatural turn in during-workout clips

## Watch Rule
Watches are disabled by default.

Watch constraints:
- no visible watch
- no Apple Watch
- no Garmin watch
- no smartwatch
- no GPS watch
- no readable watch UI
- no watch-checking pose
- no watch close-up

Exception: if the hook is explicitly about watch anxiety, one small unbranded sports watch can appear naturally. Still avoid readable UI, product close-ups, and checking-the-watch poses.

Allowed clothing rotation:
- black fitted short-sleeve running shirt
- white lightweight running singlet
- navy fitted long-sleeve performance shirt
- heather grey performance t-shirt
- olive sleeveless running top
- cream lightweight long-sleeve running shirt
- shirtless warm-weather running look

Allowed accessory rotation:
- no hat
- black running cap
- white running cap
- grey trail cap
- thin black running headband
- no glasses
- dark sport sunglasses
- brown sport sunglasses
- clear running glasses

Allowed shorts rotation:
- black split shorts
- charcoal running shorts
- cobalt blue 5-inch split shorts
- forest green running shorts
- navy running shorts
- dark green trail shorts
- burnt orange split shorts

Angle rotation:
- medium-close three-quarter post-run action angle
- side-tracking action angle
- side/back angle
- waist-up cooldown angle
- low three-quarter trail angle
- wider side angle with route depth
