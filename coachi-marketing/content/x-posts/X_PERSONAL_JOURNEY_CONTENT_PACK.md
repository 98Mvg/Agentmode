# X Personal Journey Content Pack

## 1. Brand Direction

This X account should not feel like a faceless app timeline.
It should feel like a serious person building something real in public.

Core account angle:
- AI builder
- founder / operator
- investor / allocator
- runner

Primary promise:
- follow the thinking
- follow the decisions
- follow the mistakes and corrections
- follow the road of building Coachi, not just the product itself

Coachi should appear as:
- the proving ground
- the case study
- the product being shaped in public

Coachi should not appear as:
- the entire identity of the account
- the subject of every post
- a brand voice speaking at people

Direct mention budget:
- default to `0` direct Coachi mentions in a 3-post daily set
- allow `1` direct Coachi mention only when the product name adds proof or specificity
- across any rolling `10`-day block (`30` posts), keep direct Coachi mentions to `3 to 6` posts total
- if the idea still works after removing `Coachi`, that is usually the stronger version for X

Desired account feel:
- personal
- ambitious
- builder-first
- premium
- intelligent
- clear
- native to X

Hard anti-goals:
- no fake guru tone
- no hustle-bro language
- no generic motivation
- no faceless app account energy
- no fake vulnerability for engagement
- no empty AI hype

## 5. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Building In Public
What to post:
- what changed in the product today
- what assumption broke
- what feature was cut
- what lesson became clearer after real use

Good examples:
- one filter that saved a bad feature
- one product decision that made the app simpler
- one mistake that taught a better rule

### Pillar 2: AI Workflows
What to post:
- how AI speeds things up
- where AI still fails
- what parts still require judgment
- what workflow changed the build process

Good examples:
- prompt looked good but product question was wrong
- model output was strong but timing was weak
- automation saved time but hurt quality until the guardrail changed

### Pillar 3: Founder / Operator Lessons
What to post:
- prioritization
- product scope
- user understanding
- what to ignore
- what to stop building

Good examples:
- why subtraction improved the product
- why one constraint made the product better
- why the right answer was to simplify, not expand

### Pillar 4: Investor / Allocator Thinking
What to post:
- filters
- attention allocation
- capital allocation mindset
- asymmetric decisions
- opportunity cost

Good examples:
- one good filter beats ten noisy signals
- attention is capital inside product work
- good products, like good bets, come from cleaner selection

### Pillar 5: Running / Performance Truth
What to post:
- easy-day mistakes
- pacing problems
- tension and breathing
- consistency
- progress over time

Good examples:
- why a run felt hard before the number changed
- why the route changed the reading
- why consistency matters more than one heroic day

### Pillar 6: Mistakes, Fixes, Lessons
What to post:
- what went wrong
- what fixed it
- what rule changed after the mistake

Good examples:
- a false product assumption
- a bad automation path
- a feature that sounded smart but helped no runner

## 6. Posting System

### Core Rule
The account should be followable even if someone never downloads Coachi.

### Daily Posting System
Default daily system:
1. `AI lesson`
2. `shipping lesson`
3. `runner truth`

Interpretation:
- `AI lesson` = what building with AI taught me
- `shipping lesson` = founder / operator / investor judgment
- `runner truth` = the lived human problem

### Best Daily Mix
- Post 1: AI / workflow / product insight
- Post 2: founder / allocator / decision-making lesson
- Post 3: running or performance truth tied to real life

### Weekly Content Ratio
- `40%` building + AI
- `20%` founder / operator
- `15%` investor / allocator mindset
- `25%` running / performance / user truth

### Weekly Rhythm
- `5 to 7` short posts that feel like real-time thinking
- `1` deeper thread
- `1` proof or milestone post
- `1` more personal journey post

### Product Mention Rule
- direct Coachi mention is fine
- direct Coachi explanation is not the default
- let the lesson carry the post
- let the product appear inside the lesson

### CTA Rule
- most posts should be linkless
- the profile and pinned tweet should handle most conversion
- direct CTA should be rare and earned

### Engagement Rule
Use replies and likes to build the graph around:
- builders
- founders
- investor-minded operators
- AI product people
- serious runners with strong language and taste

## 7. Tone Guide

The voice should feel like:
- a serious builder
- high-agency but calm
- ambitious without performance theater
- sharp without trying too hard
- intelligent without sounding academic
- personal without oversharing

Write like someone who:
- ships real work
- notices details
- changes their mind when reality says so
- respects attention

Sentence rules:
- strong first line
- short paragraphs
- clear rhythm
- no filler
- no hashtags by default
- no emoji unless it clearly helps
- no exclamation marks unless there is a strong reason

Avoid:
- `crushing it`
- `locked in`
- `we are so early`
- `10x`
- `game changer`
- `I am humbled and excited`
- fake inspiration
- founder cosplay

Preferred language:
- what changed
- what failed
- what became clear
- what matters
- what I cut
- what I learned
- what I am still wrong about

Brand-language rule:
- most posts should still feel complete if the app name disappears
- let Coachi show up as proof, not as filler
