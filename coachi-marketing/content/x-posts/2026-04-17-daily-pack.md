# X Daily Pack — 2026-04-17

## Post 1
Type: `AI lesson`

Noisy data makes AI sound smarter than it is.

That lesson keeps showing up while I build Coachi.

A runner can feel calm, breathe normally, and still get one ugly spike from the watch. If the model reacts too fast, the product starts coaching the spike instead of the run.

Sometimes the smartest coaching response is: do not let one number take over the story.

## Post 2
Type: `shipping lesson`

One weird signal should not hijack the whole run.

That sounds obvious until you have to ship the product.

The system has to decide when to react, when to wait, and how much certainty it has actually earned from the last few seconds of the run.

Most of the product work lives in that judgment layer.

## Post 3
Type: `runner truth`

A lot of runners do not panic because the run changed.

They panic because one number changed.

Breathing is still controlled. Legs still feel normal. Ten seconds later the brain starts acting like the whole session is broken.

A lot of easy runs get ruined by interpretation, not effort.
