Capture requirement

Record 8–10 seconds of the main workout phase with a stable connected heart-rate signal and a genuine coach cue. Keep the actual workout values and cue together. Avoid the map and personal location; do not substitute stationary warmup or add invented subtitles.

Acceptance check

Readable current iPhone UI, main workout phase, stable signal, actual cue, known build and no private location. A live run must be recorded by the runner; fixture data must be clearly labelled and cannot prove connectivity.
