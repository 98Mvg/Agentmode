## Format Notes

### Reel
- `6 to 15` seconds
- one idea only
- hook in first `0 to 2` seconds
- large overlay text

### Carousel
- `5 to 7` slides max
- slide 1 strong hook
- last slide points to Coachi

### Story
- `3 to 6` slides
- low production
- poll, update, repost, or CTA support

## Daily Loop Notes

- Reel target: `1` per day
- Story target: `3 to 6` slides per day
- Carousel target: `1` every `2 to 3` days
- Engagement target:
  - `15` likes
  - `10` meaningful comments
  - `10` follows
  - `5` replies to comments or DMs
