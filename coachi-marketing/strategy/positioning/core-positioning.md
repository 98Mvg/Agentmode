# Core Positioning

## Category
Real-Time Voice Coaching

## One-Line Positioning
Coachi is the AI running coach that listens to your body and guides you in real time, so you do not have to guess if you are doing the session right.

## What Coachi Is Not
- Not a tracking app
- Not a static training-plan app
- Not a post-run analytics dashboard

## What Coachi Owns
- Voice-first coaching during the run
- Body-aware guidance through Apple Watch heart-rate data
- Post-workout coach conversation

## Traffic Message Priority
Lead with:
1. coach in your ear
2. real-time heart-rate-aware guidance
3. confidence during the run

Avoid leading with:
1. generic AI claims
2. tracking language
3. feature lists without a coaching outcome
