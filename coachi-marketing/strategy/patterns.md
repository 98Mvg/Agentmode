# Traffic Patterns To Exploit

## Patterns That Fit Coachi

### 1. Relief Hook
Use copy that resolves uncertainty:
- "Not sure if you should slow down or push?"
- "What if your coach told you in the moment?"

### 2. Coach-In-The-Ear POV
First-person run content is the clearest bridge from awareness to click.

### 3. Beginner Permission
Permission-based content lowers resistance:
- slow is fine
- walking is fine
- restarting is fine

### 4. Apple Watch Translation
Do not sell raw heart-rate data.
Sell the interpretation:
- your watch knows your pulse
- Coachi tells you what to do with it

### 5. During-The-Run Differentiation
Competitors mostly coach before the workout or analyze after it.
Coachi owns the "during" moment.

### 6. One Post, One Runner Moment
Traffic posts perform better when they center on one specific live-running moment instead of stacking every Coachi feature in the same draft.

Use:
- one pain
- one promise
- one CTA

Best examples:
- "not sure whether to slow down or push"
- "phone stays in your pocket"
- "your coach tells you in the moment"

### 7. TikTok First, Instagram Reuse
For Coachi's free traffic model:
- build original correction content for TikTok
- reuse the strongest pieces on Instagram Reels
- use Instagram more as trust and profile conversion than as a separate ideation engine
