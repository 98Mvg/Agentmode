# Coachi Free Traffic Priority

## Objective

Rank the free traffic channels Coachi should prioritize for attention, trust, and conversion to `Coachi.no`.

This is not a generic creator-growth list.
It is tuned for:
- an AI running coach app
- beginner to intermediate runners
- busy adults
- Apple Watch and iPhone users
- traffic that should lead toward app download

## Ranked Channels

### 1. TikTok

This is Coachi's best chance at free reach.

Why it fits:
- runner mistakes are easy to package into short corrective hooks
- the category has strong self-recognition moments
- beginner runners respond to "I've been doing this wrong" framing

What Coachi should post:
- "This is why your runs feel hard"
- "Most people think they are doing Zone 2 right"
- "Your watch shows your pulse. It does not tell you what to do"
- "The mistake happens in the first 10 minutes"

Creative rule:
- lead with one mistake
- correct it fast
- sound like a coach, not a hype creator

CTA:
- `Coachi in bio`

Why it ranks first:
- highest upside for attention
- strongest fit for Coachi's corrective, coach-led content style

### 2. Instagram Reels

Instagram should reuse TikTok, not invent a second content engine.

Why it fits:
- same short-form correction content works here
- stronger trust layer than TikTok
- better profile-level conversion surface once someone checks the brand

What Coachi should do:
- repost TikTok-native runner mistake videos
- pin the clearest "start here" content
- keep profile messaging tight around `AI coach, not tracking app`

Creative rule:
- reuse the same runner face across related Instagram and TikTok organic visuals
- keep the same hook and message unless the platform context clearly demands a rewrite

Why it ranks second:
- lower creation cost because it reuses TikTok
- strong bridge from awareness to trust

### 3. SEO On Coachi.no

This is the long-term compounding traffic channel.

Why it fits:
- Coachi solves real search problems, not entertainment-only problems
- beginner runners actively search for clarity
- search traffic can move directly into app-intent pages

Best topic types:
- `Zone 2 running explained`
- `Heart rate training for beginners`
- `Why easy runs matter`
- `How to know if you are running too hard`
- `Why every run feels hard`

Each page should:
- answer one real beginner-running question
- translate confusion into simple action
- end with a direct path to Coachi

CTA:
- `Download Coachi`

Why it ranks third:
- slower than social
- stronger long-term traffic quality

### 4. App Store Optimization

ASO is free discovery from users already looking for a solution.

Why it fits:
- Coachi is an app, so App Store search is high intent
- users searching for running, pulse, and zone training are already close to action

Focus areas:
- clear keyword coverage
- screenshots that explain the benefit immediately
- benefit-first subtitle and screenshot copy

Message angle:
- `Train in the right zone automatically`
- `AI coach, not tracking app`

Why it ranks fourth:
- not a content channel
- still one of the highest-intent free acquisition surfaces

### 5. Comment Strategy

Commenting is Coachi's fastest lightweight distribution habit.

Why it fits:
- comments can turn borrowed attention into profile clicks
- running and Apple Watch content constantly creates openings for corrective replies

Where to comment:
- TikTok running videos
- Instagram running reels
- X posts about Zone 2, Apple Watch training, pacing, and consistency

How to comment:
- clarify the mistake
- keep the tone calm and coach-like
- do not sound like a founder fishing for traffic

Example:
- `Most people are not bad at Zone 2. They just do not know how to stay in it once the run starts.`

Why it ranks fifth:
- high leverage
- low production cost
- depends on consistent daily execution

### 6. Reddit

Reddit is better for insight mining and selective authority than aggressive acquisition.

Why it fits:
- runners describe their pain in plain language
- good place to pressure-test Coachi messaging
- occasional high-value posts can drive qualified clicks

How to use it:
- mine real language first
- post only when there is genuine value
- avoid obvious promotion

Best use cases:
- feedback-style posts
- educational explanation posts
- discussion threads where confusion is already present

Why it ranks sixth:
- strong audience signal
- weak if handled like a spam channel

### 7. Email Capture On Coachi.no

Email matters after traffic exists.

Why it fits:
- gives Coachi a way to convert warm visitors later
- supports education-first conversion for runners who are not ready immediately

Best lead magnet angle:
- not generic fitness advice
- something specific to beginner running clarity

Examples:
- `4-week smart start running plan`
- `How to stay in the right zone without guessing`

Why it ranks seventh:
- useful retention and conversion layer
- not a traffic source by itself

## Channel Rules

- TikTok is the primary free-reach engine.
- Instagram Reels is the reuse-and-trust layer.
- SEO is the long-term compounding layer.
- ASO is the highest-intent free discovery layer.
- Reddit is for insight first, traffic second.
- Comment strategy should be daily, not occasional.
- Email capture only works if the website already gets attention.

## Coachi-Specific Content Rules

- Do not make this sound like generic fitness creator advice.
- Lead with runner confusion, pacing mistakes, pulse confusion, and consistency problems.
- Keep Coachi positioned as `AI coach, not tracking app`.
- Optimize for:
  - attention on TikTok
  - trust on Instagram
  - intent on SEO and ASO
  - conversion on the website

## Default Execution Order

1. Build TikTok hooks around one runner mistake at a time.
2. Reuse the best TikTok pieces on Instagram Reels.
3. Turn recurring questions into SEO pages on `Coachi.no`.
4. Tighten App Store screenshots and keyword positioning.
5. Use comments daily to borrow attention from larger creators.
6. Use Reddit selectively for insight and occasional authority.
7. Capture email only after the landing page message is working.
