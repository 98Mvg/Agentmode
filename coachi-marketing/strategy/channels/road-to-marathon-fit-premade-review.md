## Rewrite Before Reuse

Do not repost these teaching-shaped premade ideas unchanged:

- `2026-07-09-marathon-01-week-2-why-every-easy-run-did-not-feel-easy`
- `2026-07-09-marathon-02-why-these-running-shoes-feel-better-on-slow-days`
- `2026-07-09-marathon-04-why-rest-days-make-long-runs-feel-easier`
- `2026-07-11-marathon-02-marathon_week_three_plan-why-runners-do-not-make-every-marathon-day-hard`
- `2026-07-11-marathon-03-marathon_first_longer_run-why-runners-keep-long-run-pace-easy-at-the-start`
- `2026-07-11-marathon-04-marathon_before_long_run_food-why-runners-fuel-before-a-long-run-even-when-pace-feels-easy`
- `2026-07-11-marathon-05-marathon_ten_minute_strength-why-runners-use-10-minute-strength-to-keep-easy-pace-easy`
- `2026-07-11-marathon-07-marathon_long_run_watch_screen-why-runners-simplify-the-watch-screen-on-long-runs`
- all 2026-07-12 Week 4 decks whose hook starts with `Why runners` or explains what runners `should` do

Rewrite them as a day in her story. For example:

- `Why runners keep long-run pace easy at the start` becomes `I went out too fast and paid for it by minute 42`.
- `Why runners simplify the watch screen` becomes `I hid half my watch data and finally stopped negotiating with every split`.
- `Why runners fuel before a long run` becomes `The breakfast I nearly skipped made the second half possible`.

## Remove From The Premade Direction

- Generic list-shaped app reviews. The only exception is the rare transparent
  personal format with the exact hook `Top 5 running apps I use when running`,
  one concrete job per app, Coachi at number `2`, and the full ranking on the
  final slide.
- Generic lessons with no first-person event.
- Repeated “best”, “rules”, or “should” hooks.
- Any Coachi mention that is not tied to a specific training problem.
