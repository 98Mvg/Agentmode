# Ideal Customer Snapshot

## Core Buyer
- Age: 28-42
- Stage: wants to become a consistent runner, but does not fully identify as "a runner" yet
- Device: iPhone first, Apple Watch highly relevant
- Geography: Norway first, English-speaking urban markets second

## Main Job To Be Done
Help me know I am doing my run correctly without needing a human coach.

## Frictions
- Does not know whether the pace is right
- Feels exposed or embarrassed by inconsistent fitness
- Gets overwhelmed by rigid plans and too much data
- Wants coaching, not tracking

## Emotional Trigger
Relief from uncertainty during a run.

## Traffic Angle
The message that pulls attention is not "more data" or "better analytics." It is:
"What if your coach was actually there with you?"
